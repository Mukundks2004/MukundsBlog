My name is Mukund. Welcome to my blog!

I started this blog in 2024 as a way to write about my degree. I learnt very quickly that I love blogging. Degree-specific content took a back seat and I started exploring other blogging genres such as personal blogging, book reviews, and technical articles. I've since written about maths, programming, university, my work, and my philosophies.

I love building things. I also like working with people to solve problems. Above all, I enjoy teaching.
