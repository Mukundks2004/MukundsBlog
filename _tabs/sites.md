Small creators have a special place in my heart. Skills such as writing, programming, drawing, and speaking, are so important to develop.

When you practice creative skills, you learn to express meaning and develop your identity. Even through simple choices, like what thumbnail you choose, or whether to order elements vertically or horizontally, or with what tone to make a certain point.

One of the greatest contributions of the internet is the voice it gives to indie artists and the power to connect them to an audience. Personal sites and blogs are a brilliant, low-pressure way to start creating.
