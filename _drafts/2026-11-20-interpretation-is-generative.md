*Yeah man, interpreting is generative*

We all know that interpreting is generative in different ways. An audience brings a perspective, a bias, a filter, a lense, to what they consume- they 'generate' a novel *interpretation*. Philosophically, I think that the act of thinking about something 'creates' an idea in this abstract space of ideas that is the human condition (? What is this collection of ideas ). An audience also adds meaning that creators welcome, and develops their community.

I want to offer a spin on this: the people who generate, who film movies, write books, do improv, they're the most auteurist interpreters. There is a bi-implication, both premises (intepreting and generation) imply both consequents (generation and skillful interpretation). This is because people who generate necessarily develop the same abilities it takes to originally interpret, their brain is altered to force them to recognise artistic choices and parallels from their own experiences. The same way that learning to drive forever changes your experience as a passenger. 

I'll go further and say that generators are nothing more than 'real' people. People who live a certain reality- artists and auteurs who are 'real'- they understand the rules of that reality better. So they are able to bend them and dance around them which makes for such rich interpretations. It is a very specific and curious expression of expertise. (what is she an expert on? life? her lives?)
